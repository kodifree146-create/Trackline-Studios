TRACKLINE STUDIOS

ABOUT THE STUDIO

Built around the railway.

Trackline Studios is a railway-focused creative studio building digital experiences inspired by the trains, routes, stations and history of New South Wales.

Explore projects → See development

WHAT WE DO

Railway experiences, built with purpose.

Trackline Studios brings together railway history, digital environments, mapping and simulation to create experiences that feel connected to the railway they represent.

01
🚆

Railway

Trains, stations, routes and railway environments form the foundation of everything we build.

02
🗺️

Geography

Railway networks are connected to the places around them. Mapping and geography help bring that connection into our work.

03
⚙️

Simulation

We explore how railway systems operate, from rolling stock and infrastructure to the environments they move through.

OUR APPROACH

More than moving trains.

A railway is a system. Every station, bridge, signal, carriage and stretch of track contributes to the experience.

01

Authenticity

We aim to represent railway environments with attention to their real-world character and history.

02

Immersion

Details matter. The atmosphere of a railway can be just as important as the railway itself.

03

Curiosity

Railways have decades of stories, engineering and changing infrastructure to discover. There's always something else to learn.

OUR FOCUS

Inspired by New South Wales.

From major railway corridors to quiet country stations, New South Wales has an enormous railway story. Trackline Studios uses that railway environment as the foundation for its digital projects.
